# Post-match review brief

You missed the outcome. The point of this review is to make the next prediction better, not to explain the result away.

Decide first: was this a reasoning error or variance?
- `reasoning`: a signal was available before kickoff (in the fact pack, in your own sources, or findable with the research the brief asks for) and you weighed it wrongly or missed it. Name the signal precisely.
- `variance`: your reasoning was sound and the match turned on events no pre-match analysis could weight higher (a red card in the first ten minutes, a deflected 90th-minute winner against the run of play). Say why you would make the same call again, and check your probabilities: if you gave the actual outcome under 20 percent, variance is a hard case to make.

Write `bodyTh` in Thai, 120 to 250 words: what you expected, what happened, where the gap came from, and what you will do differently (or not). `missedSignal` is one concrete line in Thai. `lesson` is one reusable rule under 200 characters, in Thai, or null when there is none; a lesson is a rule about how to analyse ("อย่าให้น้ำหนักกับผลลัพธ์ 5-2 มากกว่า xG เมื่อคู่แข่งเหลือสิบคน"), never a restatement of the score.
